# Frozen jq Goldens

Every file here was generated by running homebase `runners/lib/email.jq` at commit `2b04a5a3`, the jq library `src/email/` was ported from, with synthetic arguments. The names and numbers are invented; nothing here came from a runner's real output.

Regenerating one against a newer jq would defeat the point. These are frozen: they say what the email looked like the day the port was made, and a component that stops matching one has drifted from the design, not from the fixture. Changing the design means changing both sides deliberately.

Four deliberate departures. On 2026-09-24 the runner emails went mobile first and `StatStrip` changed to wrapping inline block cells, so its goldens were rewritten from the component. In 8.0.0 `StatStrip`, `MonoTable`, `WhatMoved`, and `RecordTimelineLegend` were removed from the package, their goldens deleted, and their rows cut out of `page-full.html` by hand, so that file is the jq composition minus those four. Also in 8.0.0 the palette's `faint` went from `#8a8272` to `#736c5f`, since the jq value is 3.38 to 1 on the ground at 11px, under text contrast; every golden here and `../frozen/failure-page-plain.html` had the literal swapped and nothing else.

The fourth, later in 8.0.0, came from the browser stories, and each file was edited by rule rather than regenerated, so its diff shows these changes and nothing else. Words now pass 4.5 to 1 on the paper and the ground: `faint` moved again, to the brand kit's `#6e6757` (4.99 on the ground); small accent words (`color:#FC4A1A`, 3.31 on paper) became the palette's `accentInk`, `#B8300B`, as did the boxes of `Badge` and the New pill (`border:1px solid` and `border:1.5px solid`), while the accent stays on the marks with no words (the masthead rule, a link's underline, a callout's edge); and `warm` went from `#B7791F` (3.52) to `#966319` (4.96). A long token no longer widens the email past a phone: the cells of `Row`, `Item`, `Note`, `ListRow`, `ReadBlock`, a `DayStrip` day, a left aligned `Records` cell, and the plain failure page's log gained `overflow-wrap:anywhere;word-break:break-word`. `Records` became a data table read by column: its table lost `role="presentation"`, its header cells became `th scope="col"` with `font-weight:normal` so they look the same, and a column's pixel width became its share of 630 (130 is `20.63%`). The pieces that drew meaning with no words gained a text alternative: a lone `Bar` is `role="img"` named "2 of 3", while a `TargetRow`'s bar is unchanged beside its count. The jq has none of these; they are this package's, on purpose.

The `.html` files are compared after DOM normalization (`../normalize.ts`), never byte for byte: React writes `&#x27;` where jq writes `&#39;`, `<br/>` where jq writes `<br>`, and an explicit `<tbody>` where jq leaves the parser to insert one. The `text/` files are compared byte for byte, because a column that drifts by a space is the failure the plain text part exists to prevent.

The generating command, for the record:

```bash
jq -rn -L <homebase>/runners/lib 'include "email"; <call>'
```
