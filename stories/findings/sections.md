# Findings: Sections

The bugs the sections' stories exposed (`stories/sections/`), each fixed in `src/` by a later pass. Written only by the sections' writer; the format is in `stories/README.md`. Ratios are on the stories' theme, the practice's palette in `.storybook/theme.css`: `--surface` `#f6f1e7`, `--surface-alt` `#efe8da`, `--card` `#fdfbf6`, `--surface-inset` `#e9e0cd`, and the quiet gray `--neutral-500` `#736c5f`.

A cause shared across components carries one id, whatever components show it, so the fix that ends the cause ends its marker everywhere at once: `muted-text-contrast` is the quiet gray wherever it falls short, and `inset-soft-text-contrast` the soft words on the inset well.

## Accessibility

- `muted-text-contrast`: `BlogIndex`, `BlogPost`, `LegalPage`, `LocationSection` (through its hours table), and `ReviewCarousel`, each in `AltSurface`. Rule `color-contrast`. The quiet gray, `--neutral-500` (`#736c5f`), as small text on `--surface-alt`: 4.26:1 at 12.8 to 15.2px, under 4.5:1. It clears 4.5:1 on `--surface` (4.62) and `--card` (5.03), so every story on those grounds passes. It is a closed day's hours (13.6 and 15.2px), the journal's dates and reading times (12.8px), a post's way back, byline, and dates (13.6px), a legal page's dateline (13.6px), and the carousel's stat labels (12.8px).
- `inset-soft-text-contrast`: `MapEmbed` (`Phone`), `LocationSection` (`EmbedFacade`), `Locations` (`EmbedFacade`), `Gallery` (`Placeholders`). Rule `color-contrast`. Soft words on the inset well, `--neutral-600` (`#6b6559`) on `--surface-inset`: 4.4:1 at 12.8 and 13px. They are the map facade's "Tap to load the map" and a gallery tile's shot brief.
- `review-score-out-contrast`: `ReviewCarousel` (every story with a denominator). Rule `color-contrast`. The "/ 5" beside the score, `--neutral-300` (`#b6a98f`): 2.05:1 on `--surface` at 25.6px (19.2px on a phone and a tablet) and 1.9:1 on `--surface-alt`, under the 3:1 large text floor.
- `form-error-contrast`: `ContactForm` (`Validation`, `RelayRefuses`, `NowhereToSend`). Rule `color-contrast`. The error words in `--primary-deep` (`#d63a10`), which #80 left off the new ink: a refused field's message 4.17:1 on `--surface` at 13.1px, and the send failure 3.96:1 on `--primary-tint` (`#fee7df`) at 17px. `--primary-ink` would clear both.
- `map-facade-name-mismatch`: `MapEmbed` (`Phone`), `LocationSection` (`EmbedFacade`), `Locations` (`EmbedFacade`). Rule `label-content-name-mismatch`. The facade's name, "Load the map to Kestrel Hollow Bakehouse, 418 Alder Street, ...", does not contain its visible words, which lead with the address and end "Tap to load the map", so a reader who presses by voice, saying what they see, misses it.
- `process-step-heading-order`: `ProcessSteps` (every story). Rule `heading-order`. Each step's title is an `h4` under the band's `h2`.
- `location-hours-heading-order`: `LocationSection` (every story). Rule `heading-order`. The Hours heading is an `h4` under the section's `h2`.

## Behavior

- `marquee-reads-twice`: `LogoMarquee` (`Default`, `ReducedMotion`). The track repeats every chip for the seamless loop and nothing hides the copy, so a screen reader reads each credential twice (two matches per credential in the band). Under reduced motion the row stops and wraps, and the copy is still drawn, so the still block shows every chip twice on the page.
- `heading-long-word-overflow`: `PageHero` (`UnbrokenWord`), `BlogPost` (`UnbrokenWord`), `BlogIndex` (`UnbrokenWord`), `FeatureGrid` (`UnbrokenWord`). A 47 letter word in a heading never breaks, so on a 390 phone the page scrolls sideways: `scrollWidth` 1006 (the page hero's `h1`), 800 (the post's `h1`), 513 (an archive entry's `h2`), 629 (a card's `h3`). Reproduce: any of those stories, `document.documentElement.scrollWidth > window.innerWidth`. Headings have no `overflow-wrap`, and the tracks around them keep `min-width: auto` (rule 5).
- `credential-long-word-overflow`: `CredentialList` (`UnbrokenWord`). The same word in a credential's title: `scrollWidth` 549 at 390. The row's text column is a flex item with `min-width: auto`.
- `feature-detail-long-word-overflow`: `FeatureDetail` (`UnbrokenTag`). The same word as a tag chip: `scrollWidth` 408 at 390.
- `split-long-word-overflow`: `SplitSection` (`LongAddressPhone`). A 69 character email address in a column's paragraph, the realistic case: `scrollWidth` 592 at 390. Neither the column nor the paragraph breaks it. `ContactInfoCard` already solves the same thing with `min-width: 0` and `overflow-wrap: anywhere`.
- `expand-long-word-overflow`: `ExpandList` (`UnbrokenWord`). The word in a row's title: `scrollWidth` 467 at 390.
- `profile-long-word-overflow`: `ProfileCards` (`UnbrokenHandle`). A long handle in the mono line under a name: `scrollWidth` 537 at 390.
- `review-long-word-overflow`: `ReviewWall` (`UnbrokenName`). The word as a reviewer's name: `scrollWidth` 471 at 390.
- `map-static-dead-link`: `MapEmbed` (`StaticNoLink`). A static screenshot with no `mapLinkURL` is still wrapped in a link to `#`, named "Get directions to ...", that goes nowhere but the top of the page. With no link it should be a plain picture.
- `jump-rail-no-current`: `JumpRail` (`FollowsScroll`), and the FAQ's rail with it. The marked tab differs by color and underline alone; no tab carries `aria-current`, so a screen reader is never told where on the page it is.
- `contact-status-unannounced`: `ContactForm` (`SendsAndThanks`). The thank you and the send failure are plain blocks: neither is a live region, and focus stays on the button, so a screen reader hears nothing after a send. Expected: `getByRole("status")` holding the message (rule 14 allows `<output>` or `aria-live` for a one shot status).
- `contact-errors-unannounced`: `ContactForm` (`Validation`). A refused field shows its message, but the input carries no `aria-invalid` and is not described by the message (`aria-describedby`), so a screen reader hears neither that it was refused nor why.
- `reveal-slider-home-end`: `RevealSlider` (`Keyboard`). The handle is a `slider` and answers only the left and right arrows. Home and End (and the up and down arrows, and Page Up and Page Down) do nothing, where the platform's slider convention moves to the ends.
- `carousel-dots-ignore-reduced-motion`: `ReviewCarousel` (`ReducedMotion`). Under reduced motion the slide's fade is off, but each step still starts four transitions on the dots (their width and background color, a quarter second each), which have no reduced motion rule (rule 13). The story listens for each transition as it starts, since on a loaded machine a quarter second can end before a check that looks for running ones.
- `faq-clear-drops-focus`: `FAQBrowser` (`Search`). The clear button removes itself once the search is empty, and the focus it held goes with it, to the page's body, rather than back to the search field it cleared.

## Observed, Not Yet Marked

- `LogoMarquee` scrolls forever on a 32 second loop with no way to pause it; only the reduced motion setting stops it. WCAG 2.2.2 asks for a pause on moving content that runs past five seconds.
- `Gallery` keys a tile by `src ?? placeholder ?? caption`, so two tiles showing the same photo, or sharing a brief, collide, and a tile with none of the three gets an undefined key. It opens no lightbox, so there is no open and close to test.
- Rule 19 (a component never sets a column width): `PricingCard` caps itself at 520px (`.cost-card`), `PricingBand`'s lead at 48ch, `Locations`' lead at 58ch inline, and `Gallery`'s lead at 60ch inline.
- Rule 12 (`target="_blank"` only through `newTabProps`): `Locations`' directions link, `ReviewCarousel`'s profile caption, and `ReviewWall`'s date link each write the attribute by hand. Each takes an http link today, so nothing misbehaves yet.
- The prime directive (no site copy in a component): many sections carry fixed English the page cannot change, visible and accessible alike. `FAQBrowser` (Search questions, Clear search, Expand all, Collapse all, Back to top, Copy link to this question, Link copied, the no match sentence), `ContactForm` (every label, every error, Send Message, Sending), `MapEmbed` (Get Directions, Tap to load the map), `LocationSection` (Proudly serving, Hours), `Locations` (Directions), `ContactInfoCard` (Call / Text, Email, Visit, Portal), `ReviewCarousel` (Previous review, Go to review N, Review N of M), `ReviewWall` (Read more, Show less, both control labels, Newest, Oldest), `LegalPage` (Last updated), `BlogIndex` (min read), `BlogPost` (Updated).
- `ContactInfoCard` finds its Portal row by searching `footerLinks` for a label containing "portal": any footer link so named is promoted into the card, and a portal named otherwise never shows.
- `ContactForm` uses fixed ids (`contact-name` and the rest), so two forms on one page share ids and every label points at the first form.
- `ContactForm` leaves focus on the button after a refused send, where moving it to the first refused field is the usual help.
- `RevealSlider` reports a bare number (`aria-valuenow`) with no `aria-valuetext`, so a screen reader hears "50" with no word for what it measures.
- `JumpRail` with every stop filtered away (the FAQ with no match) still draws an empty navigation landmark.
- `ReviewCarousel`'s dots are 8 by 8 px targets; axe's target size rule passed them (their spacing may carry them), unverified by hand.
- `FullBleedVideo`'s `PlaysOnScreen` (not this pass's story) failed in about a third of the whole group and whole suite runs on this shared machine, on its wait for the clip's opacity to reach 1, and passes alone. `chrome/BrandLockup` (`WideWordmarkImage`) and `chrome/SiteShell` (`FullPagePhone`) did the same in the whole suite runs.

## Checker Limits (Not Bugs)

- `FullBleedVideo`'s fetch margin: a story runs in an iframe, which clips what lies past its own edge, so an observer's `rootMargin` never reaches beyond it. The clip's arrival within a screen of the viewport cannot be shown in a story; its arrival on screen is (`PosterFirst`).
- Reduced motion in a stylesheet: answering `matchMedia` in script never reaches a sheet's `@media (prefers-reduced-motion)`, and setting the reader's preference through the test browser reaches every frame of the test page, so stories in other files running beside it would lose their motion too. The sections' stories copy the loaded sheets' reduced motion rules, unconditioned and in their own layers, into the story's own document, and answer `matchMedia` there (`stories/sections/motion.ts`).
- `userEvent.tab()` ignores `inert`: it moves focus to a link inside a closed `ExpandList` or `FAQBrowser` body, which the browser refuses, so focus stays put. A closed body is checked by asking its link for focus and watching it refuse; Tab is walked only through open bodies.
- A fragment navigation inside the test frame (following a `#` link) closes the test run's browser connection. `FAQBrowser`'s `CategoryJump` and `BrowseAll` hold the click and scroll the target into view the way the browser does, its scroll margin honored.
- A frame's own navigation is not listed in the parent page's resource timing, so `MapEmbed` proves the embed was not loaded before the press by counting the page's frames as well as its fetches. The embed is a local stand in (`/images/sections/map-embed.html`), so the press never reaches a map service.
- `ContactForm`'s mail handoff assigns `window.location`, which a story cannot follow or intercept, so `MailHandoff` shows the form only.
- A dialog's `close` event lands a task after the dialog shuts. `BlogPost`'s figure story waits for the large picture to unmount before pressing the tile again; a press in between is undone by the late event. No reader presses that fast.
