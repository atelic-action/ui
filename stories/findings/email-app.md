# Findings: Email and App

The bugs the stories of the email kit and the app shell exposed (`stories/email/`, `stories/app/`), each fixed in `src/` by a later pass. Written only by that group's writer; the format is in `stories/README.md`. App ratios are on the shell's light theme in `styles/app.css`; email ratios are on the atelic palette (`src/tokens/palettes.ts`).

## Accessibility

- Email faint ink: `Masthead` (every story), and every email piece that sets words in `faint`: `Eyebrow` (all but `Strong`), `Footer`, `TitleCard`, `SubEyebrow`, `StatsRow` and `Stat` (`WithCaption`), `Note`, `Fold`, `Item` (its fold's summary), `TargetRow`'s note, `DayStrip`'s day labels, `RecordStack`'s meta, `Records`' header and muted cells, `RecordTimelineStrip`'s axis, `FailurePage`'s eyebrows, and `WeeklyReport` (`MailClient`, `Phone`); all marked `a11y: { test: "todo" }`, because an inline styled row carries no class to scope a rule by. Rule `color-contrast`. The atelic palette's `faint` (`#8a8272`) is 3.38:1 on the `ground` (`#f6f1e7`) and 3.68:1 on the `paper` (`#fdfbf6`), at 9 to 13px everywhere it is used. `#6e6757` is 4.99:1 on the ground and 5.43:1 on the paper; the `ClientPalette` story shows the pieces pass whole once the palette's inks are dark enough (a faint of `#5f665a` is 5.28:1 on the ground).
- Email accent ink: `TitleLine` and `Item` (the value on the right), `TargetRow` (`Short`), `GroupRow`, `ReadBlock` ("The Read"), `Note` (`Accented`), `RecordStack` (the New pill, the callout's eyebrow), `Records` (a hot cell), `FailurePage` (the fix's label), `BigFold` once opened onto an item, `WeeklyReport`; marked as above. Rule `color-contrast`. The `accent` (`#fc4a1a`) set as small words is 3.31:1 on the paper and 3.04:1 on the ground, at 10 to 13px. The accent works as a mark (the masthead rule, the timeline's open dots, a link's underline) and fails as text; a darker text accent (`#c2360f` is 5.3:1 on the paper and 4.87:1 on the ground) or the ink for the words with the accent kept for the mark fixes it. A third tone fails the same way: `RecordStack`'s warm aside number (`#b7791f`, 3.52:1 on the paper).
- `app-destructive-contrast`: `ActionButton` (`Destructive`), `PageError` (every story), `AppLayout` (`UserMenuOpen`, `MobileMenuOpen`), `UI/Button` (`Destructive`), `UI/Badge` (`Destructive`, `AllVariants`), `UI/Alert` (`Destructive`), `UI/Form` (`SubmitEmpty`, `ErrorsPhone`). Rule `color-contrast`. The kit's `--destructive` (`0 84.2% 60.2%`, `#ef4444`) fails both ways it is used: the destructive button and badge's `--destructive-foreground` (`#fafafa`) on it at 3.6:1 (14px), and `text-destructive` words on the card and the ground at 3.76:1 and 3.6:1: `PageError`'s title, the Log out row in the user menu and the menu sheet, the destructive alert, and a form field's label and message once it is invalid (3.63:1 on `#fbfbf9`). A darker destructive token (red 600, `#dc2626`, is 4.83:1 under white and 4.63:1 under `#fafafa`) fixes every use.
- `app-muted-on-grey-contrast`: `ErrorFallback` (`WithDetails`, `NotAnError`, `LongDetailsPhone`), `UI/Command` (`InADialogOpen`), `UI/CountryDropdown` (`FlagsFromAnOutsideHost`, `OpenPhone`). Rule `color-contrast`. `--muted-foreground` (`#71717a`) on the kit's grey (`--muted` and `--accent`, both `#f4f4f5`): 4.39:1. The error fallback's details box at 12px; a highlighted command item's shortcut at 12px; the country list's highlighted "Not specified" at 14px. Darkening `--muted-foreground` a step (`#6b6b73` is 4.81:1 on the grey) fixes all three.
- `error-details-not-focusable`: `ErrorFallback` (`LongDetailsPhone`). Rule `scrollable-region-focusable`. The details `pre` scrolls (`max-h-40 overflow-auto`) but takes no focus, so a keyboard user cannot scroll a long stack. `tabIndex={0}` with a label fixes it.
- `user-menu-name-mismatch`: `AppLayout` (every desktop story: `DesktopRail`, `CollapseTheRail`, `CollapsedRail`, `UserMenuByClick`, `UserMenuOpen`, `LongUserName`). Rule `label-content-name-mismatch`. The rail's user menu trigger shows the user's name (expanded) or initials (collapsed) and is named "User menu" by `aria-label`, which replaces what is seen: a voice user saying "Wren Okafor" reaches nothing. Drop the `aria-label` when the name shows, or make it "Wren Okafor, user menu".
- `collapsed-rail-links-unnamed`: `AppLayout` (`CollapsedRail`, and `CollapseTheRail` as a behavior check). Rule `link-name`. Collapsed, `DesktopRail`'s `RailLink` drops the label span and the link carries no name; the tooltip only describes it, and only while open. Every section link in a collapsed rail is an unnamed link. An `aria-label={item.label}` on the collapsed link fixes it.
- `alert-title-heading-level`: `UI/Alert` (`OnAPage`). Rule `heading-order`. `AlertTitle` is always an `h5`, so an alert under a page's `h1` skips three levels. A heading level prop (or a plain `p` with the title's style, since an alert's title is rarely a section) fixes it.
- `command-dialog-unnamed`: `UI/Command` (`InADialog`, `InADialogOpen`). Rule `aria-dialog-name`. `CommandDialog` takes only `DialogProps` and renders `DialogContent` with no `DialogTitle`, so the palette's dialog has no name, and Radix logs its missing title warning. A `title` prop rendered as a visually hidden `DialogTitle` fixes it.
- `command-separator-in-listbox`: `UI/Command` (`Arrows`, `Empty`, `InADialog`, `InADialogOpen`: every story where the separator shows; it hides once filtering empties a group). Rule `aria-required-children`. A `CommandSeparator` is a `separator` inside cmdk's `listbox`, which may hold only options and groups.
- `command-empty-listbox`: `UI/Command` (`Empty`). Rule `aria-required-children`. With nothing matching, the `listbox` holds no option: the empty line is a sibling of the options, not one of them.
- `country-popover-unnamed`: `UI/CountryDropdown` (`ChosenThenCleared`, `FlagsFromAnOutsideHost`, `OpenPhone`). Rule `aria-dialog-name`. The popover content is a `dialog` and the dropdown gives it no name. An `aria-label` ("Countries") on its `PopoverContent` fixes it.
- `country-list-holds-the-search`: `UI/CountryDropdown` (the same stories). Rule `aria-required-children`. The dropdown sets its `CommandInput` (a `combobox`) in a sticky block inside `CommandList` (the `listbox`). Moving the input above the list, as `Command` itself lays it out, fixes it.
- `pagination-prev-name`: `UI/Pagination` (every story that draws it). Rule `label-content-name-mismatch`. The previous button shows "Prev" and is named "Previous page", which does not hold the word on screen. Show "Previous", or drop the `aria-label` (Next is fine: "Next" is in "Next page").
- `slider-thumb-unnamed`: `UI/Slider` (every story). Rule `aria-input-field-name`. The wrapper renders `SliderPrimitive.Thumb` with no props, so the `slider` a reader reaches (the thumb) can never be named: an `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby` on `Slider` lands on the root, not the thumb. Forwarding an `aria-label` (or `thumbProps`) to the thumb fixes it.
- `toast-rich-colors-contrast`: `AppProvider` (`SuccessToast`, `ErrorToast`). Rule `color-contrast`. The provider's default toaster is sonner with `richColors`, whose success words (`#008a2e` on `#ecfdf3`) are 4.25:1 and error words (`#e60000` on `#fff0f0`) 4.34:1 at 13px, title and description alike. Dropping `richColors` (sonner's plain toast passes) or restyling the tones through the toaster's `toastOptions` fixes it; an app can pass its own `toaster` meanwhile.
- `toolbar-action-time-name`: `AppPageToolbar` (`ActionWithTime`). Rule `label-content-name-mismatch`. A toolbar action is always given `aria-label={ariaLabel ?? label}`, so its visible trailing time (`ago`, "06:02") is cut from its name. The label alone needs no `aria-label`; set one only for an icon only action.

## Behavior

- `toolbar-clear-drops-focus`: `AppPageToolbar` (`ClearByKeyboard`). Tab to the search's clear button and press Enter: the box empties and the button removes itself, so focus falls to the page instead of returning to the search box. Reproduce: a toolbar with a search value, focus the clear button, Enter; `document.activeElement` is the body. The clear handler should focus the input.
- `toolbar-notice-beside-row`: `AppPageToolbar` (`WithNotice`). The `notice` is documented as "a status line below the toolbar row", and on a phone it is; on a desktop `.ap-tools` is one wrapping flex row, so the notice sits beside the search, between it and the filter. Reproduce: a toolbar with a search, a filter, and a notice at 1280; the notice's top is above the search's bottom. Probably an omission (the notice wants `flex-basis: 100%` or an order after the selects); confirm the intent before fixing.
- `error-details-widen-page`: `ErrorFallback` (`LongDetailsPhone`). With `showDetails`, a stack whose longest line is longer than a phone (a request path) widens the centred column to 419px at 390, so the page scrolls sideways: the column is a flex item at `min-width: auto` and the `pre`'s line sets it (component rule 5). `min-w-0` on the column, or `w-full` on it, lets the `pre` scroll inside itself.
- `skeleton-says-nothing`: `PageSkeleton` (`AnnouncesLoading`). The skeleton is bare blocks with no `aria-busy`, no status, and no words, so a screen reader hears nothing while a page loads. An `aria-busy="true"` with a visually hidden "Loading" fixes it.
- `command-dialog-drops-focus`: `UI/Command` (`InADialog`). `CommandDialog` has no trigger of its own (it is opened by state, the way a palette is), and Radix's dialog restores focus only to a `DialogTrigger`, so closing it drops focus to the page instead of the control that opened it. Reproduce: open it from a button by state, Escape; `document.activeElement` is the body. Passing focus back in `onCloseAutoFocus`, or letting the dialog take a trigger, fixes it.
- `country-flags-from-outside-host`: `UI/CountryDropdown` (`FlagsFromAnOutsideHost`). Opening the list asks for one flag image per country, 255 of them, from `react-circle-flags.pages.dev`, the library's default `cdnUrl`: every visitor who opens it tells a third party host about the visit, and with that host blocked or offline every flag is a broken image. The stories serve a local stand in and count what was asked for. Serving the flags from the app (the library takes a `cdnUrl`, which the dropdown does not pass) fixes it.
- `select-list-wider-than-trigger`: `UI/OptionSelect` (`Phone`). The popper list is the trigger's width plus its own border and its viewport's padding (`min-w-[var(--radix-select-trigger-width)]` on the padded viewport), so it overhangs the trigger's right edge; at full width on a phone it runs 12px off the screen (right edge at 402 at 390). Putting the minimum width on the content rather than the padded viewport fixes it.
- `search-clear-drops-focus`: `UI/SearchInput` (`ClearByKeyboard`). The same as the toolbar's: Tab to the clear button, Enter, and the button removes itself, dropping focus to the page. A press with the mouse keeps focus in the box (the button prevents its own mousedown), so only the keyboard path loses it.
- `progress-value-dropped`: `UI/Progress` (`AnnouncesItsValue`). The wrapper takes `value` out of the props to draw the fill and never passes it to `ProgressPrimitive.Root`, so the `progressbar` has no `aria-valuenow` and reports itself indeterminate: a screen reader hears no progress at all. Passing `value` through fixes it.
- `form-describedby-dangles`: `UI/Form` (`DescribedByAMissingId`). `FormControl` always puts the description's id in `aria-describedby`, even for a field with no `FormDescription`, so the reference points at nothing (the Trays field in the stories). Harmless to a reader today, but it is an invalid reference a checker may flag; build the list from the parts that rendered.
- `plain-failure-log-overflows`: `FailurePage` (`PlainLongPathPhone`). The plain failure page's log `pre` sets `white-space: pre-wrap` and nothing else, so a long path with no hyphen in it (`/srv/bakehouse/runners/weekly_ops/cache/ledger_2026W47_wholesale_accounts_export.json`) cannot break and runs past the card on a phone. The structured page's log, the same `pre`, carries `overflowWrap: anywhere` and fits (`StructuredLongPathPhone`); the plain one wants the same `wrapLong`.
- `email-long-word-widens`: `Row` (`LongWordPhone`). No email row sets `overflow-wrap`, so one long unbroken token in a row (an address, a path) holds its cell open and the whole email grows wider than the phone: a 390 phone gets a 381px column where 366 fits. `FailurePage`'s structured block and `RecordStack`'s title already wrap (`overflowWrap: anywhere`, `wordBreak: break-word`); the same on `Row`, `Item`, `Note`, and `ListRow` fixes it.
- `day-strip-word-widens`: `DayStrip` (`LongNamesPhone`). Seven days across a phone leave each about 45px, and an entry word wider than that ("sourdough" at 12px is about 55px) cannot wrap inside its day, so the strip widens the email to 414px at 390. Short names fit (`ShortNamesPhone`). Allowing a word to break in a day's cell (`overflowWrap: anywhere`), or a smaller type size below a width, fixes it; or the runner keeps entries to a short word.
- `records-fixed-widths-overflow`: `Records` (`FixedWidthsPhone`). Column `width`s fix the table's layout, and widths chosen for a desktop column (here 440px across four) hold the table open on a phone, so the email runs off the screen. A width set as a share of the table rather than in pixels, or a `max-width: 100%` on a fixed table, keeps sibling tables on one grid without breaking a phone.
- `records-header-not-th`: `Records` (`HeaderCellsAreHeaders`). The header row is plain `td`s, so a screen reader reading a number cannot say which column it is in. `th scope="col"` for the header cells fixes it in every client that reads tables.

## Observed, Not Yet Marked

- `PageError` is not an alert or a status (`NotAnnounced` asserts it is neither), so a reader who triggered the load hears nothing when it fails. Whether a page error should be announced is a design call; it is not marked as a bug.
- `PageSkeleton` pulses with `animate-pulse` whatever `prefers-reduced-motion` says (component rule 13). The test run does not emulate the media query, so no story shows it.
- `UI/Alert` is always `role="alert"`, a live region, so a calm notice on page load (`Default`) is announced as urgent. A status role for the default variant is likely what is meant.
- `UI/Card`'s `CardTitle` is a `div`, not a heading (`Composed` asserts the card holds none), so a page of cards gives a screen reader nothing to move between.
- `UI/BadgeList` truncates a long tag at `max-w-24` with no title or tooltip (`LongTag`), so a sighted reader sees "Seventy tw…" and cannot read the rest.
- `UI/CountryDropdown`'s flags are images with a `title` of the lowercase code ("pt") and no `alt`, so each country is announced with its code before its name. They are decorative and want `alt=""`.
- `ActionButton` sets `aria-label={label}` at every width, though the label is visible from `sm` up. Harmless while the two agree (`OwnAccessibleName` shows an `aria-label` must keep the label's words, or axe flags the mismatch).
- `Badge` (email) and `RecordTimelineStrip` draw meaning a reader never gets: a badge is one letter with nothing saying what it stands for, and a timeline's sends and opens are drawn boxes with no words; only its axis ("30d", "today") is read. A text twin exists for the plain text part (`recordTimelineText`), not for the html one.
- `Bar` has no words at all; the count beside it in a `TargetRow` carries the meaning, so a `Bar` used alone says nothing to a reader.

## Checker Limits (Not Bugs)

- `checker-radix-hides-page`: `AppLayout` (`UserMenuOpen`), `UI/DropdownMenu` (`CheckAndChoose`, `Open`), `UI/Select` (`Open`), `UI/OptionSelect` (`Phone`). An open modal Radix menu or select hides the rest of the page with `aria-hidden` (each element marked `data-aria-hidden`) and holds focus inside itself, so nothing hidden can be reached; axe still counts the hidden page's links as focusable content inside an `aria-hidden` region (`aria-hidden-focus`).
- A story that fails part way while a Radix layer is open leaves the next stories in the same file unable to close theirs: the exit animation never ends and the closed layer stays mounted. Seen only while a file had failures; with every story green each closes as it should.
- Email `Badge`: its letter is the accent at 9px on a 1px accent border, 3.31:1 on the paper, which fails WCAG, yet axe does not report it (with the check set to fail, no `Badge` story does), so the stories carry no marker. Measured by hand from the palette.
- The sent document (`WeeklyReport`'s `SentDocument` and `SentDocumentPhone`) renders in an iframe from `renderEmail`, and the check does not reach into the frame: the same faint and accent failures are in it, unreported. The stories assert its layout (no sideways scroll at 760 and 366) and the composed stories outside a frame carry the contrast.
